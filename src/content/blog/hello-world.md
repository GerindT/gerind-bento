## What you'll find

Notes on things I ran into while building software. Mostly backend, some frontend, a fair amount of deployment, and the occasional bug I caused myself. I've been doing this for about five years, long enough to have opinions and short enough that they still change every few months.

The first posts after this one are about this very site. A missing title tag, a clock that showed the wrong time, a server I didn't need. Small stories, but real ones, and I'd rather write about what actually went wrong than pretend it went smoothly.

## What you won't

"10 tips to become a better developer." Docs rewritten with a different font. Anything that starts with "In today's fast-paced world."
