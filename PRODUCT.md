# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing: Astro 4 (static output), UnoCSS, Solid (globe, tooltip), Svelte (playground), motion, d3. Deployed on Cloudflare (Workers static assets) at gerind.dev; the old gerindtershana.netlify.app redirects there.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and employers deciding in seconds whether to contact Gerind. Also freelance clients scoping a project, and fellow developers and peers who browse for personality and craft. Confirmed by the owner as "both, plus peers".

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Gerind Tershana, software engineer in Albania (5+ years, full-stack: React, Vue, Nuxt, Node.js, Go, Laravel, Python; Lead System Architect, previously at Detag). It exists to prove what he has built, make contacting him or downloading his CV effortless, and show the person behind the work.

## Positioning

A portfolio that is itself a crafted piece of work: stylish, opinionated and playful, never a template bento. The personal side (media taste, places visited, switchable themes, hidden GIF easter egg) is part of the pitch.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, a CV or a shared link, on desktop and phone. Three jobs: see projects, get the CV or contact details, get a feel for the person.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home (calling card, menu, selected work, contact), /projects (grouped tabs: Personal & freelance, Innovation4Albania, Detag, Friends' sites, Early days), /about (profile, career, globe), /media (books, movies, manga), /photos (only when photos exist). The blog was removed in Nov 2026; /blog/* redirects to /projects.
- Keep: visitor-selectable accent theme picker, GIF easter-egg switcher on the intro, globe of visited countries, media page.
- Contacts: gerindt@gmail.com, GitHub GerindT, LinkedIn gerindtershana, Instagram gerindtershana, Telegram GerindT.
- CVs: English and Albanian PDFs in /public (last updated November 2024 per current copy; confirm before reuse).
- Projects carry status (live, concept, private, dev only), year and stack. No outcome claims or metrics exist yet; do not invent them.
- Local time and the header date block use Europe/Tirane.
- Innovation4Albania and Detag work is shown with names, stack and a factual line, linked only where the product is already public (pasqyraecmimeve.al, mycred.it, muse-x.al, reginagroup.al). Each project and media item opens a calling-card detail overlay (`?p=<slug>`).

## Brand Commitments

Owner-pinned visual direction: inspired by the Persona 5 video game, "stylish and clean", explicitly not a basic portfolio. The owner wants the designer's own take offered as well. No reproduction of the game's logos, characters, or artwork.

## Evidence on Hand

Real project screenshots in /public/illustrations/projects (live-site captures from Nov 2026 plus store art for Vanspot and After Hours; Pasqyra, MyCredit and Regina travel captures added Nov 7 2026; Globe ERP, Tetris and the other I4A tools have no images), movie/book/manga covers in /public/illustrations, GIFs of the owner, two CV PDFs, a "Detag" logo. No testimonials, metrics or case studies exist; do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Proof of work before personality, but personality is what makes it memorable.
2. Contact and CV are always one action away.
3. Delight never blocks content: no loader gates, no hover-only essentials.
4. Never claim what is not true: no invented clients, numbers or testimonials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: contrast, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion respected, no hover-only links.
